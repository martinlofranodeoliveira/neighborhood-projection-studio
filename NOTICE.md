retains its own terms. The package retains actual legal texts under
`neighborhood_projection_studio/_notices/`: direct wheel members and conservative
base/prior materials for the genuine pinned Python closure, including build/test

qualified one Linux x86_64 CPython3.13.18 stack, NumPy2.5.4, SciPy1.18.1,
scikit-learn1.9.1 and threadpoolctl3.8.0, with setuptools84.2.1/wheel0.48.0.
